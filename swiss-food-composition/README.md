# Data & License Information

This directory contains the source food composition data and translations used to build the app's
food database.

## Source Data

**Swiss Food Composition Database**  Federal Food Safety and Veterinary Office
FSVO https://naehrwertdaten.ch/en

Published on opendata.swiss under its open terms of use, which allow commercial use with attribution
of the source: https://opendata.swiss/en/terms-of-use#terms_by

Downloaded from https://opendata.swiss/en/dataset/naehrwerte_lebensmittel

## Translations

The localized food names are translated for Food You by community contributors.

## License

This dataset operates under a mixed licensing structure:

- **Original Food & Nutritional Data:** Owned by the FSVO and used under the `opendata.swiss` terms
  of use.
- **Translations:** Licensed under the project's license. As translated names reference and derive
  from FSVO entries, FSVO source attribution still applies to the overall database.
